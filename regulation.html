03 — Perspective

What regulation does, and doesn't

It can mean

  • ✓The firm is supervised and must meet conduct and capital rules.
  • ✓You have a formal route to complain beyond the company.
  • ✓Records and client-money handling can be inspected.

It does not mean

  • ✕Your trades will be profitable. Leveraged products can lose money quickly.
  • ✕Every product is covered. Permissions differ by licence and country.
  • ✕A mentor's results are verified or guaranteed.
